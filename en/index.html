FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Real free?

Yes. You can use Real with daily free keys. Plus and Pro keys are optional paid access from verified resellers for no ad-steps and extra perks.

Where do I download Real?

Use the official Download page on projectreal.gg. Avoid reposted installers, modified archives, or files sent through direct messages.

How do Plus and Pro work?

Plus and Pro are redeemed with license keys, not subscriptions. They add access time and unlock plan perks on the Real account where you redeem them.

Can I use Real on a new PC?

Yes. Reset the old device from the Dashboard or during app login, then sign in on the new PC. Device reset cooldowns depend on your plan.

Why does Windows block the installer?

Windows or browser security can warn about protected installers. The Installation guide explains how to prepare Windows Security and keep the official RealSetup.exe.

Where do I get support?

Join the Real Discord, link your account when needed, and open a support ticket with screenshots or logs so the team can help faster.
